Privacy Policy - last updated 2026-09-15

Private by design

The EcoChineur browser extension collects no personal data, browsing data, history, Vinted searches, filters, identifiers or usage statistics.

No data use or sharing

EcoChineur does not use, sell or transfer user data for any purpose: no analytics, profiling, advertising, audience measurement, creditworthiness, model training or sharing with third parties.

Local storage only

The only stored information is what you choose in the popup: brand categories, materials and language. It stays in chrome.storage.local on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the extension.

No network

The extension makes no network requests of its own, has no backend, loads no remote code and includes no tracking, telemetry or analytics.

How the extension works

When you open the popup and click "Apply filters" or "Reset", EcoChineur reads the active tab URL only to check that it is a Vinted catalog page and to build, on your device, a new URL that keeps your existing filters. The tab then navigates to that URL. The URL is never stored or sent anywhere.

The "Suggest a brand" link opens the project's public GitHub issue form in a new tab. Submitting it requires a GitHub account, and GitHub's privacy policy applies there.

Permissions

storage

Save your preferences locally on your device.

activeTab

Read and update the active tab URL, only after you open the popup. No access to other tabs or sites.
