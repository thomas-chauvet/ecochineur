Suggest a brand

Know a brand that belongs here?

No account needed. A maintainer checks every suggestion and records the evidence before a brand joins the list.

1. Vinted must know it

EcoChineur filters with Vinted's own brand IDs, so the brand has to appear in the Brand filter of a Vinted search. If you opened this page from the extension while filtering on that brand, its ID is already filled in.

2. Pick the closest category

  • Made in France (with or without Origine France Garantie)
  • Made in Europe, outside France
  • France plus other European countries only
  • Eco-conscious, produced anywhere

3. Link the evidence

Production sites, certifications (GOTS, B Corp, Fair Wear…), repair programs or environmental reports. Claims we cannot check are not listed.

The form is not available right now. Suggest the brand on GitHub instead (GitHub account required).

The form is hosted by Tally (Belgium). It only receives what you type, plus the Vinted brand ID and domain shown in the form. Prefer GitHub? Open an issue.
